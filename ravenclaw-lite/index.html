CLA3129 Week 10 — Estimating the lost first segment of the Peutinger Map

Exercise

  1. Toggle on the TP Image layer to see the surviving map. How many parchment sheets can you identify from the scroll boundaries?
  2. Count the TP toponyms (red dots) per sheet. Use this as your per-sheet average.
  3. Note how many RC littoral places are not on the surviving TP (orange triangles). These are candidates for the missing western section.
  4. Using the per-sheet average and the missing-section count, estimate how many sheets are missing — and where Rome would fall on the complete map.
