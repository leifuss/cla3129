Ptolemy's regional alignments

About this visualization

This map shows the 95 regional boundaries from Ptolemy's Geographike Hyphegesis (2nd century CE), plotted in Ptolemy's own coordinate system. Longitude runs from 0 (the Blessed Isles) to 180 degrees (Sera/China).

The red meridian lines represent hour intervals of equinoctial time (1 hour = 15°). Sub-intervals at 20-minute spacing (5°) are shown as dashed lines.

Use the meridian slider to shift the grid eastward. At approximately 5° (20 min), the meridians align with many European regional boundary edges.

The Asia and Africa sliders shift those continental blocks independently. The paper finds Asia aligns best at ~1° east, and Africa at ~1.25° east.

Toggle Marinos' critiques to see the measurement lines Ptolemy disputed, and the Carthage-Arbela eclipse observation that anchored his longitudinal framework.
